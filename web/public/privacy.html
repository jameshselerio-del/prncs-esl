Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Komorebi English is a booking site for one-to-one English classes with a single teacher. This policy explains what information the site collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. See also our Terms of Service.

Information we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in with Google: your name and email address. We do not receive your Google password and do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts.
  • Profile details you enter: name, time zone, English level, native language, learning goals and an optional contact (for example a phone number or messaging ID).
  • Booking information: the classes you book or cancel, their times, the topic and notes you add, and cancellation reasons.
  • Class records kept by the teacher: lesson materials, feedback that you can see, and private notes about your classes that only the teacher can see.

How we use it

  • To create your account and show you the right classes, times and lessons.
  • To schedule classes and prevent double bookings.
  • To send you emails about your classes: booking and cancellation confirmations, calendar invitations and reminders.
  • To let the teacher prepare lessons and give you feedback.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Google user data

The site's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google name and email are used only to sign you in, identify your bookings and email you about your classes.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Your information is stored in a Google Sheet in the site owner's Google Drive and processed by a Google Apps Script that runs under the owner's Google account. Emails are sent through Google.
  • The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.
  • Only the teacher and the site administrator can see student information. Other students cannot see your details or your bookings.
  • We do not share your information with anyone else, except where required by law. Google and GitHub process data only as service providers for hosting, storage and email.

Information stored in your browser

While you are signed in, the site keeps your Google sign-in token in your browser's session storage, which is cleared when you close the tab or sign out. It also remembers your preferred booking view (table, calendar or timeline). The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active so that your class history and feedback stay available. You can ask us at any time to correct or delete your information; we will delete your profile and booking history within 30 days, except where we must keep a record by law.

Your choices

  • You can update your profile at any time from the Profile page.
  • You can cancel classes within the cancellation policy shown when you book.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, or for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing us (below).
  • You can remove the site's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

Students under 16 should use the site with the permission and involvement of a parent or guardian, who may contact us about their child's information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced by email to registered students.
